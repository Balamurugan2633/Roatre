FREQUENTLY ASKED

Answers, before you ask.

Quick clarity on the most common questions we receive — for everything else, the form above is the fastest way to reach us.

How quickly can my academy get started with Roatre?

Most academies are onboarded within a week. After your first demo we set up a tailored workspace with your teams, players, and reporting templates ready to go.

Do you offer training for coaches and staff?

Yes — every plan includes hands-on onboarding sessions, structured training material, and ongoing support so your team uses Roatre confidently from day one.

Is Roatre a good fit for individual athletes and parents?

Absolutely. Roatre is built for the entire development ecosystem — athletes get clear performance visibility, and parents stay connected to the journey through transparent reports.

What sports does Roatre support?

Roatre is sport-agnostic by design. Our analysis frameworks adapt to football, cricket, basketball, tennis, and more. Reach out and we'll walk you through what's already configured for your sport.

How is my data protected?

Player and academy data is private by default, hosted securely, and only visible to authorized users in your organization. We're happy to share a full security overview on request.
